  "claim": "The sky is blue due to Rayleigh scattering.",
  "citation_id": "cit_001",
  "page": 1,
  "paragraph": 1
}

What changed: the citation marker "[1]" was removed from the claim
text, but preserved as a separate link via citation_id.
