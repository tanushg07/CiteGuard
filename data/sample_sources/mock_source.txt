Title: Evaluation of Large Language Models on Scientific NLI

Abstract:
In this paper, we present our findings on verifying scientific claims using NLI. Our final evaluation showed an accuracy of 95.2% on the held-out test data. This is a significant improvement over the baseline model, which only achieved 88.4%.

Methodology:
We trained a cross-encoder using the RoBERTa-large architecture. The dataset consisted of 10,000 claim-evidence pairs.

Conclusion:
NLI is highly effective for evidence verification.
